Akathist to St. Daniel the Prophet

Served on the feast of the Holy Prophet Daniel (December 17) and whenever appointed.

The file also contains the Prayer of Azariah and a Canon of Prayer to the Holy Prophet Daniel, which are not included here.

Apolytikion — Tone 2

Great are the achievements of faith! In the fountain of flame, as by the water of rest, the Three Holy Children rejoiced. And the Prophet Daniel proved a shepherd of lions as of sheep. By their prayers, O Christ our God, save our souls.

Kontakion — Tone 3

Since thy pure and hallowed heart had been made pure by the Spirit, it became His dwelling-place and clearest prophecy's vessel; for thou didst behold things far off as near and present, and when cast into the den, thou didst muzzle lions; for which cause, O blessed Prophet, glorious Daniel, we all revere thee in faith.

Readers

Kontakion 1

Chosen vessel of God and lampstand of the Unapproachable Light you have shown yourself to the world, Holy Prophet Daniel, having been enabled by your holy life to prophesy the things to come. For this, as an icon of the Divine mysteries and organ of the Holy Spirit, we honor you, singing: Alleluia!

Ikos 1

Born of royal lineage in Vitora above, you were taken as a slave in your childhood from Judea to Babylon with Jehoiakim King of Judea. And the gift of God was poured into your heart from a young age. By your wonderful judgment you delivered Jehoiakim’s wife, the pure Susanna, from death at the hands of the unjust and unfaithful judges. For this we cry to you:

Rejoice, Thou overflowing dawn of wisdom

Rejoice, Thou from infancy honored by the elders

Rejoice, Thou knower of hidden things

Rejoice, Thou balance of divine justice

Rejoice, Thou stretched out bow of God’s right hand

Rejoice, Bearer of heavenly light

Rejoice, Pillar of virginity and all wisdom

Rejoice, Thou swift helper of those who trust in the Lord

Rejoice, That His Law has been revealed in your living heart

Rejoice, O lamp that dispels the darkness of ignorance

Rejoice, for thou uncoverest and shamest all wicked counsel

Rejoice, Thou flame of the Spirit that burnest the servants of the passions

Rejoice, Holy Prophet Daniel, thou made wise by God

Kontakion 2

Beautiful in appearance and skillful in all wisdom, knowledgeable in all knowledge. With deep insight and full of zeal, you were chosen to serve in the palace of the king of Babylon, along with the three young men, Anania, Azariah and Mishail. And you, like them, guarding the purity of your life and faith, served the Lord with all your heart, to who you sang unceasingly, Alleluia!

Ikos 2

The beauty that springs from the love of God shone on your face and on the faces of the three young Saints, Holy Prophet Daniel, when with the fast of a nourishing soul you overwhelmed the young men eating at the kings’s table. For after ten day looking more beautiful and fat in body than these, the head of the families permitted you to keep yourselves in the blessed Law.

Rejoice, Flower of fasting and pure prayer

Rejoice, Thou valiant, self-denier for the glory of God

Rejoice, Steadfast faithfulness in His Word

Rejoice, Worship, flight of heart to heaven

Rejoice, Heart wrapped in Divine longing

Rejoice, Unveiled crown

Rejoice, For choosing the narrow way, you have clothed yourself with power from above

Rejoice, You delight in spiritual food

Rejoice, Thou fulfillment of God’s will

Rejoice, Victory over lust and insanity

Rejoice, Deliverance from their terrible bondage

Rejoice, Strength of faith and hope in God

Rejoice, Holy Prophet Daniel, thou made wise by God

Kontakion 3

The blessing of God was poured out upon you and the three young men, for He gave you knowledge and skill in all writing , as well as abundance of wisdom, and you, Holy Prophet Daniel, were able to interpret visions and dreams in the light of the Holy Spirit, in Whom you sang: Alleluia!

Ikos 3

No one did the king find wiser than you and Ananias, Azariah and Mishail. In all the questions on which he asked for guidance, when it came to wisdom and skill, he found you ten times smarter than the magicians and soothsayers in the whole kingdom.

Rejoice, Thou mind filled with the power of the Spirit

Rejoice, Thou flourishing meadow of wisdom

Rejoice, Thou treasury of God

Rejoice, High place of the knowledge of God

Rejoice, Thou deep of humility

Rejoice, Fountain of ever-flowing grace

Rejoice, The height of human wisdom

Rejoice, Shame of false wisdom

Rejoice, Word equally bright with wondrous life

Rejoice, Thou speedy helper in the straightening of lives

Rejoice, Lever of wisdom that overcomes error

Rejoice, Spiritual guide in the labyrinth of passions

Rejoice, Holy Prophet Daniel, thou made wise by God

Kontakion 4

A dream troubled the spirit of King Nebuchadnezzar, so that he commanded all the wise men to perish, unless they told him the dream and its interpretation. All the sign-tellers and pagan sorcerers were left as speechless fishes, only you, Holy Prophet Daniel, boldly asked for time to reveal the mystery. And having told your friends Ananias, Azariah and Mishail, you earnestly prayed for God’s mercy and sang together: Alleluia!

Ikos 4

Then the mystery was revealed to you, Holy One, in a vision of the night, and you praised the Almighty. Thou didst see the Blessed, Pure Virgin from whom was cut, not by hand, the Chief Cornerstone, Christ God. And behold, the stone crushed the image with the head of gold, the breast and arms of silver, the belly and thighs of brass, the feet one part of iron and the other of clay, the image of deceit hidden in the temporal. And the stone that became a high mountain and filled the earth, you have made clear as the coming of the Kingdom of God.

Rejoice, herald of the Incarnation of Christ

Rejoice, That you have foretold the birth of God from the Virgin

Rejoice, Word painter of the Divine mysteries

Rejoice, O beacon who unveils the thoughts of the heart

Rejoice, Thou stone of the knowledge of God

Rejoice, Thou sight of those above the world

Rejoice, Pen of the Spirit who writes with skill

Rejoice, You who nourish the Church with heavenly bread

Rejoice, Son and heir of the Kingdom

Rejoice, Beauty of the righteous honor of God

Rejoice, Thou ripe grape that bringest forth the wine of immortality

Rejoice, Thou guide in secret things

Rejoice, Holy Prophet Daniel, thou made wise by God

Kontakion 5

The emperor fell on his face and bowed down before you, Holy Prophet Daniel, and confessed that your God is the God of gods, and the Lord of kings, the Revealer of mysteries, the One to whom it is fitting to sing the holy hymn: Alleluia!

Ikos 5

Neither you nor the three young men, who, having been accused, were thrown into the fire for confessing the right faith, worshipped the golden image made by the king. But the fire turned into dew at the coming of the Son of God, at whose sight you rejoiced in spirit, praising and blessing Him

Rejoice, Most holy confessor

Rejoice, Thou perfector of the faith

Rejoice, Infused icon with Grace

Rejoice, Thou skillful helmsman of the powers of the soul

Rejoice, Thou guide of us to the shore of repentance

Rejoice, Cup of wisdom and love of God

Rejoice, That thou givest it to us who thirst

Rejoice, Burning bush of God’s grace

Rejoice, That the coming of Christ has quenched the veil of sin with the dew of eternity

Rejoice, Thou scatterer of vanity of the world’s glory

Rejoice, Olive branch borne by the dove of the Spirit

Rejoice, O prayer for deliverance from the furnace of temptation

Rejoice, Holy Prophet Daniel, thou made wise by God

Kontakion 6

Thou has prophesied to Nebuchadnezzar, O holy Daniel, that he should be driven from among men, and dwell among the beasts of the field, and be like them seven years, until he should know that the Most High hath dominion over the kingdoms. And the king, not having redeemed his sins by deeds of justice and mercy, the prophecy was fulfilled to the letter. After this time, his mind came back to him and he went up again to the sanctuary, offering blessing, praise and worship to the Lord and singing: Alleluia!

Ikos 6

King Balthazar dishonored the holy vessels taken from the Temple of Jerusalem and worshipped the foolish idols. Then the fingers of a man’s hand appeared to him, writing before the candlestick on the palace wall. Disturbed in spirit, and as none of his wise men could read the writing, nor make known its meaning, he asked you. And you prophesied to him the speedy end of himself and of the kingdom, for the pride of his heart, though he knew what God had done with his father, Nebuchadnezzar.

Rejoice, O chastisement of those who trust proudly in their own power

Rejoice, Golden chain of obedience to God

Rejoice, That thou spreadest to us the garment of humility

Rejoice, Thou boldness of prayer

Rejoice, Call to justice and mercy

Rejoice, Thou light out of the hardness of spirit

Rejoice, Lift up your eyes to heaven with the gift of the Lord

Rejoice, Though hast made they mind the house of passionlessness

Rejoice, Blessedness of God’s long suffering

Rejoice, Healing rain of pure prayer

Rejoice, Sword in the hand of the Word of God

Rejoice, Holy Prophet Daniel, thou made wise by God

Kontakion 7

In the time of Darius the Mede, on the advice of the impious rulers, a foolish command was given that anyone who prayed for thirty days to any god or man but the king should be thrown into the lion’s den. But you, Holy Prophet, before the open window towards Jerusalem, every day you knelt three times, prayed and praised God as before, incessantly chanting: Alleluia!

Ikos 7

The governors, finding good reason, compelled Darius, at last because he did not want to lose you, Holy One, and you were thrown into the den of lions, which the king sealed, hoping that the God whom you worshipped and served would deliver you. And in the morning he ran to the pit and found you unharmed, for the Angel of the Lord had sealed the lions’ mouths. Then the king, rejoicing, confessed in writing to all nations of the almighty power and eternal glory of the living God in heaven and on earth.

Rejoice, pure sacrifice to God

Rejoice, Heart made an altar

Rejoice, Unceasing remembrance of the Lord

Rejoice, That by your thrice kneeling, you showed forth the right glorification of the Holy Trinity

Rejoice, Turn of the mind to the Risen One

Rejoice, Deliverance from the pit of sins

Rejoice, That you shut the mouths of unseen enemies who would tear us apart

Rejoice, Shepherd of lions and joint inhabitant with angels

Rejoice, Thou fortified city of the Spirit

Rejoice, Good news of the glory of God

Rejoice, Thou river of wondrous healings

Rejoice, Holy Prophet Daniel, thou made wise by God

Kontakion 8

A fearful vision appeared to you, Holy Prophet Daniel, in Babylon, of the kingdoms that were to be, imagined by beasts, then of the Antichrist, the end of the ages and the fearful judgment. Thus hast thou been enabled to behold the Ancient of Days sitting on the throne of greatness, in garments white as snow, the hair of His head pure as wool, His throne flames of fire, His wheels burning fire. A river of fire poured out and come forth from Him; thousand of thousands served Him, and myriads stood before Him singing: Alleluia!

Ikos 8

The Supreme and Righteous Judge sat down, and the books were opened, and the beast with the blasphemous mouth, which had been given power to oppress the saints of God, desired to change the holy feasts and commandments, was slain, and its body was cast into the fire. And behold, in the clouds of heaven, came One like the Son of Man, to Whom was given by the Father the dominion and the glory and the kingdom forever and ever.

Rejoice, Treasure of spiritual awakening

Rejoice, That you show us the fear of God as the beginning of wisdom

Rejoice, That its end is love and suffering for Him

Rejoice, Thou fountain of healing of hidden passions

Rejoice, Love as clear as gold in a melting pot

Rejoice, Forerunner of hidden mysteries

Rejoice, Helper in unseen war

Rejoice, Flower of the Tree of Life

Rejoice, That thou has drunk of the living water of the knowledge of God

Rejoice, That thou has known the Father and the Son are one

Rejoice, Pure mirror of the Spirit

Rejoice, Trumpet of love of the Holy Trinity

Rejoice, Holy Prophet Daniel, thou made wise by God

Kontakion 9

Saint Archangel Gabriel has interpreted to you two other frightening visions about the mysteries of salvation and the wrath of God to come. The coming of the Lord Jesus Christ and the sufferings of the blameless One, the sealing of the prophecy and the fulfillment of the Law, the destruction of the Temple and the gifts of grace above the mind, the coming of the Antichrist in the time of the consummation of sins, the transgression of the holy and true and the war he will make on the saints, how in time of peace he will strike down many chosen ones and will be destroyed not by human hands. For these, with fear and trembling we worship God, chanting: Alleluia!

Ikos 9

He Himself who had the form of the Son of Man gave you strength to endure the brightness of His glory. He has revealed to you how the scoffer of the holy will be given power= to put the abomination of desolation in place of the daily sacrifice, persecuting, killing or deceiving many of the elect so that they may be discerned, cleansed and made white until the end of time. Then shall his terrible end come, when the great prince Michael shall appear, and there shall be great distress, but the righteous and faithful people shall be saved. And those who sleep in the dust of the earth will rise, some to eternal life, others to shame and eternal suffering. And the wise will shine like the brightness of heaven, and those who have guided many in the path of righteousness will be like the stars forever and ever.

Rejoice, O light of the Church, who dispels the darkness of deceit

Rejoice, Thou guardian of the heart from the vanity of sin

Rejoice, That we understand the proud thought as the face of the abomination of desolation

Rejoice, Thou flood of tears and worship that banishes the hypocritical mind

Rejoice, Thou steadfast fasting and prayer for the Lord’s mercy

Rejoice, Healing and comfort that come from suffering for Christ

Rejoice, Bearing the cross and following Christ

Rejoice, Freedom from the snares of deceitful sin

Rejoice, That being warred against by passions, we seek peace from above

Rejoice, Chosen virtue in deed and word

Rejoice, For the most precious gift that keeps the world is the Holy Sacrifice of the Lord Jesus

Rejoice, Holy Prophet Daniel, thou made wise by God

Kontakion 10

The idol Bel, to which the Babylonians offered sacrifice, you have broken, seeing the deceit of the heathen priests. For by sprinkling ashes, you showed the traces of their footsteps in the temple sealed by the kings, and the hidden doors were found through which they entered and ate the food on the table. Then King Cyrus knew that Bel was not alive, but clay and brass, and he marveled at your wisdom, which came from the One to whom you had always sung: Alleluia!

Ikos 10

But even a dragon that was considered a god you killed without sword and staff, throwing lumps of resin, tallow and hair into his mouth, cooked together. By this you showed everyone that, though great and fearsome, he was only a wicked construction, not worthy of worship.

Rejoice, Seal of spiritual awakening

Rejoice, That you sprinkle on our souls the ashes of repentance

Rejoice, That we see in it the traces of unseen thieves

Rejoice, That we find those who eat the fruit of our labors

Rejoice, That you make known to us our hidden wounds

Rejoice, That we run to Christ in faith for healing

Rejoice, That you intercede for our forgiveness

Rejoice, Praying heart, window to God’s Light

Rejoice, Banishing the hidden dragon

Rejoice, That you teach us the craft of the Spirit

Rejoice, Entrance into eternity through the gates of humility

Rejoice, Thou who hast understanding from above

Rejoice, Holy Prophet Daniel, thou made wise by God

Kontakion 11

Grieving over the loss of Bel’s priests and the dragon, the Babylonians forced the king in mortal terror to give you to them. And they threw you into a pit with seven hungry lions, and you were there seven days. And the Angel of the Lord took Habakkuk the Prophet up by his head, and led him away to Babylon in the swiftness of his spirit. And Habakkuk cried out, ”Daniel! Daniel! Take the food that God has sent you.” And you blessed Him who does not forsake nor forget those who seek Him, chanting: Alleluia!

Ikos 11

You got up and ate, and the Angel of the Lord too Habakkuk back to Judea. And on the seventh day the king came to mourn for you, and when he saw you sitting, he cried out, “Great are you, O Lord, God of Daniel! And there is none besides Thee.” And he drew you out of the pit, and those who would have killed you he threw into the pit, and the lions ate them before him.

Rejoice, O brightness of the prophets

Rejoice, You who are served by the holy angels

Rejoice, Love that seeks not its own

Rejoice, Lamb of Christ, conqueror of death

Rejoice, Shield of steadfast faith

Rejoice, Thou blessed orchard of fearlessness

Rejoice, Blessing on the despairing world

Rejoice, Thou ark of hope, that standest against the flood of temptation

Rejoice, Praise and thanksgiving for the goodness of God

Rejoice, That thou teachest us to reign with mind over the lions of passions

Rejoice, Conscience undeceived by cunning thoughts

Rejoice, O torch of the Spirit, who cheers the souls of the faithful

Rejoice, Holy Prophet Daniel, thou made wise by God

Kontakion 12

Together with Ananias, Azariah, Mishail, you reached a ripe old age, in which you suffered the cutting off of heads by the wicked emperor Atticus. When he went to cut off Ananias’ head, Azariah spread out his coat and received it. Likewise, Azariah received Mishails, and you received Azariah’s, Holy Prophet Daniel, then they cut off your head and you sang with the angelic hosts: Alleluia!

Ikos 12

And when they had cut off your head, they stuck to your bodies, and the Angel of the Lord took your bodies and carried them to Mount Geval (Byblos) near Tyre, and put them under the rock. And after the resurrection of the Lord, you rose with the three young men and showed yourselves to many. Then again you fell asleep, going up to the heavenly places prepared for you by the Father.

Rejoice, rebuke of unbelief and martyrian boldness

Rejoice, Thou shattering of the snares of sin

Rejoice, Confessor of the Resurrection of Christ

Rejoice, Chosen vessel, who reveres in the world the fragrance of the Bridegroom

Rejoice, Link in the golden chain of the Church

Rejoice, Icon of heavenly beauty

Rejoice, You who are the example of the willing sufferers for the love of God

Rejoice, Pure wheat in the Lord’s threshing floor

Rejoice, Heart that rests in the love of the Father for the Son

Rejoice, Thou inhabitant of heaven and the worlds vindicator

Rejoice, Thou conqueror of the old man

Rejoice, Name of a worshipper, worthy to be remembered

Rejoice, Holy Prophet Daniel, thou made wise by God

Kontakion 13

(Three times)

O holy Prophet Daniel, pray to God for us, to grant us tears of repentance and deliverance from the bondage of the passions, that we may know the love and freedom given by Christ. Lean into our helplessness and be our intercessor and helper, so that, as we pass through the sea of this life, the face of God’s only-begotten Son may be impressed on our faces with its radiance and beauty. And then, Holy One, may we find mercy at the Fiery Judgment, partaking of eternal life and rejoicing with the angels chanting: Alleluia!
