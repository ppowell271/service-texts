Akathist to St. Maximos the Hut-Burner

Served on the feast of St. Maximos the Hut-Burner (Kavsokalyves) of Mount Athos (January 13) and whenever appointed.

From a booklet composed by Daniel Powell; its table of contents, icon credits, scripture and historical references, and acknowledgements are not included here.

Apolytikion — Plagal of Tone One

From your mother’s womb, O Venerable Maximos, you were chosen as a treasury by God, were made worthy of the divine darkness as Moses, and to see things far off as Samuel; you are the divine wonder of Athos, the initiate of the Theotokos, O Father, who intercedes for us.

Troparion — Tone 8

By a flood of tears you made the desert fertile, and your longing for God brought forth fruits in abundance. By the radiance of miracles you illumined the whole universe! O our holy father Maximus, pray to Christ our God to save our souls!

Synaxarion

Maximos surpassed all in virtue. Although in time he labored, now he is in the eschaton.

Hymn of Praise of St. Maximos the Hut-Burner

(by St. Nikolai of Zicha)

Prayer in the heart beats as a heart, Prayer in the heart, together with breathing, Internal prayer, the light from within, On Athos, was manifested by Maximus. As a spirit without a body, Maximus was uplifted, From prayer, completely radiated with light; From prayer, was filled with joy From prayer, was filled with satisfaction Through prayer, saw the heavens opened. Through prayer, the human being was glorified, By prayer, felt the nearness of Christ, The Holy All-Pure One openly appeared to him. With heaven the soul of Maximus was sated. Gregory of Sinai once asked him: "Tell me, O righteous Maximus, from where do you know That you have good and not evil visions, And that all of these are not illusions of the devil, False temptations and Satan's deceptions?" "From this, I know," says he, "that they are not lies, That these visions, the spirit and body console, That my spirit always yearns after them That, from the sign of the cross, they will not vanish, By sweet joy, a temptation, I know it is not, By blessed joy that warms me completely."

Akathist Commemorating Saint Maximos the Hut-Burner (Kavsokalyves) of Mount Athos Commemorated January 13

Kontakion 1

O luminary of the Church, chosen by the Lord in your youth, blessed Hut-Burner Maximos, a Divine visionary and inspiring ascetic and humble servant of Jesus Christ! We praise you with love and honor for the struggles and labors you showed forth in seeking of the Heavenly Kingdom. You took not refuge in the house of man, but as Abraham you sought the City Whose Builder and Maker is God. Wandering and abiding in seclusion, you tore down all obstacles that obscured your vision of God and Heaven and traced a path of salvation with a Divinely-Energized life. As you now stand before the throne of God, pray for us who cry to you:

Rejoice, O holy Maximos, Hut-Burner, seer and abider of Heaven!

Ikos 1

Seeking Divinely endowed purity, and possessed of great love for the Lord, you fled earthly riches and took up the struggle of monastic life, O all-wondrous Hut-Burning Maximos. Guided by the Theotokos, you put first the Kingdom of God and His righteousness. Wherefore, we cry out to you with love:

Rejoice, Gifted by God in noetic prayer from your youth;

Rejoice, Forsaker of earthly riches for the Pearl of Great Price!

Rejoice, Practicer of obedience to Elder Mark of Macedonia;

Rejoice, Humble, submissive servant to Christ!

Rejoice, Our Lord’s Mother enlightened you at the entrance of Blachernae Church;

Rejoice, Entreator of the Theotokos!

Rejoice, You whose heart was warmed by the Pillar of fire;

Rejoice, Endowed with Holy visions of the Pure Virgin!

Rejoice, Revealer of visions to Elder Elias of Carmel;

Rejoice, Meek one, though foolish before men, wisely you sought to inherit the Kingdom!

Rejoice, Humble one, by carefully hiding your righteousness before men, you showed the foolishness of God is wiser than men!

Rejoice, Understanding practitioner of the paths of Onuphrius and Anthony in the desert life;

Rejoice, Emulator of the enlightened way of Peter the Athonite hermit!

Rejoice, Austere Wanderer, who like Moses, was guided by Pillar of Fire and Cloud of Smoke;

Rejoice, Virtuous leader, for after wandering like valiant Joshua and Caleb, you entered the Promised Land!

Rejoice, O holy Maximos, Hut-Burner, seer and abider of Heaven!

Kontakion 2

O luminary of the Church, chosen by the Lord in your youth, blessed Hut-burner Maximos, in holiness you soared to the heavens. As a Divine visionary, inspiring ascetic and humble servant of Christ, you condescend to our prayer. Seeing that all earthly pleasures and abodes are mingled with distractions, O holy Maximos, you despised the vanity of vanities and rejected all the beauties of this world, committing yourself wholly unto God, and chanting to Him: Alleluia!

Ikos 2

Gifted by God from childhood with an understanding of the True Path, you embarked upon that Path leading from the temporal to the eternal by fixing the heart, mind, strength and soul upon a heavenly vision. And seeing your example, we cry out to you with compunction:

Rejoice, Saint who ceased not seeking until finding Him, in whom the Law and Prophets were fulfilled;

Rejoice, Wise Saint who shows us that all earthly good things are transitory and pass quickly away!

Rejoice, Saint who warns us that all the beauties of this world are like a flower of the field;

Rejoice, Holy man who adjures us not to seek the glory of men, which is like a fleeting shadow!

Rejoice, Saint who waters the desert with the tears of longing for the eternal garden of God;

Rejoice, Sojourner who strives to turn us away from the ephemeral world to everlasting blessings of Heaven!

Rejoice, Saint who removed all obstructions from your buildings and allowed Christ to build anew;

Rejoice, Wanderer who understood that foxes have holes, birds have nests, but man’s soul must rest in Christ!

Rejoice, Saint, as one instructed by the Spirit-led Paul, as an ever moving maker of tents;

Rejoice, Saint who ever urges us to have the Lord alone as our abode!

Rejoice, O holy Maximos, Hut-Burner, seer and abider of Heaven!

Kontakion 3

The power of God made of you a human vessel of Light to enlighten mankind, O Holy Maximos, and He set you upon a holy hill that He should not be hid. He gave you to the people as a luminary to those in darkness, that they may see Light so they may unceasingly cry to the Lord: Alleluia!

Ikos 3

Having the Lord alone in your heart, mind and soul, O friend of God, you built with strength a temple more enduring than that of Solomon so that the King of Glory may come in. Knowing that God is everywhere present and filling all things and not limited to a place, you continued to tear down and build anew. And we who seek to rightly build in Godly labor, speak of these wonders:

Rejoice, Light tower that gives hope and safe guidance to God’s children;

Rejoice, All-radiant beacon of Divine Energies!

Rejoice, You who understood the Heavens are a throne and the earth a footstool;

Rejoice, Explainer that man was not made to abide in the passable work of his hands, but to abide in Christ!

Rejoice, Follower of Christ, as one who has no place to rest his head;

Rejoice, Traveler, like the Magi, toward the humble stable!

Rejoice, Craftsman-Carpenter as Joseph the Betrothed, and seer of the Theotokos;

Rejoice, You, who like the Carpenter’s Son, taught others to participate in rightly building toward eternity!

Rejoice, As the Shepherd of Hermas, you labored to shape living stones useful to the Craftsman;

Rejoice, You who built not more, larger barns, but ever prepared for repose divine!

Rejoice, When you built up and tore down, the Master Craftsman revealed Himself in each labor of humility;

Rejoice, You who looked beyond the mortal, temporal building by hands, to Him Who makes all things new and eternal!

Rejoice, You who saw the ladder used for hut construction was not the ladder to heaven;

Rejoice, You who ascended to the ladder’s highest rung described by the Climacus!

Rejoice, Friend of man, as one who climbed the ladder and tore off the roof of a house;

Rejoice, Servant, in your ascent allowing no encumbrance to lowering the afflicted to the presence of Christ!

Rejoice, Bold builder, who built upon the Chief Cornerstone;

Rejoice, Receiver, as Solomon, from David’s storehouse to wisely build a worthy temple within thy soul!

Rejoice, You who understand the tearing down of glorious temporal abodes;

Rejoice, You who knew the building back in three days!

Rejoice, O holy Maximos, Hut-Burner, seer and abider of Heaven!

Kontakion 4

Seeking not to be known, but to know God, you heeded the Divine vision of the Theotokos and set yourself on a path as Abraham, and built as Jacob to mark holy places then moved on as one seeking to ascend the Ladder. You desired to build, not in vain, but in the fashion of the Lord Who built the heavens and earth. As you built and removed all obstacles and allowed yourself to be fashioned in His image and likeness, you found that God built you a mansion. Thus we apprentices marvel and chant unto God: Alleluia!

Ikos 4

When people heard that you made your abode at a place, they hastened to you and sought your Godly counsel, asking you as a friend of God to pray for them and guide them to salvation and the Heavenly Father’s Mansion. And we, also seeking your Godly counsel, offer such praises as these:

Rejoice, Friend of God, who like Abraham, who sought a City whose builder and maker is God;

Rejoice, Whom others, as the three angels, sought you out for hospitality!

Rejoice, You who sought not an earthly abode, but, as Jacob, proclaimed the Ladder of Ascent;

Rejoice, Seer who understood that habitations in kingdoms of the world shall perish, but the Habitation of God is eternal!

Rejoice, Divine Friend of Gregory of Sinai;

Rejoice, Hearkener of the Sinaite’s counsel to let God’s Light be seen from the city on a hill!

Rejoice, Deemed by the Sinaite as a messenger likened unto the holy angels;

Rejoice, Cave-Dweller near Elder Isaiah!

Rejoice, Contemporary of Gregory Palamas, as one knowing and known by the Giver of Light and Life;

Rejoice, Tireless preacher of Orthodoxy as the right glory bestowed by God on man!

Rejoice, Wise Counselor to those empowered by God to lead the nations;

Rejoice, Deep well of humility and meekness, which gives drink to those who thirst!

Rejoice, Good lover of abstinence;

Rejoice, Storehouse feeding all with spiritual food from the Bread of Life!

Rejoice, Ever-flowing fountain of love from Divine knowledge of God;

Rejoice, Earnest Care-Provider for the salvation of men’s souls!

Rejoice, Blossom of life, springing forth from wood huts as from the Rod of Aaron;

Rejoice, Lily of paradise sprung forth in the desert and flowering by the Rose of Sharon!

Rejoice, O holy Maximos, Hut-Burner, seer and abider of Heaven!

Kontakion 5

You were shown to be a Divinely-Guided luminary from heaven in the flesh, O bright light of Christ. You abided on land elevated to heavenly light. Amid spiritual and bodily struggles, you were a good model for the Christ-loving brethren who looked upon you as a beacon. You teach all to chant unceasingly to the Lord: Alleluia!

Ikos 5

Brethren approached you and were edified by your labors. Seeing you build anew for each spiritual season, removing the obstructing roof as did the friends of the paralytic, we who need healing are brought to Christ without obstruction. And, honoring you as one truly chosen of God, we cry out to you such things as these:

Rejoice, Sanctified One who understood the earth is the Lord’s and the fullness thereof;

Rejoice, Saint who had clean hands and pure heart!

Rejoice, Saint who has not lifted up your soul in vanity;

Rejoice, Grace-Filled One who ascended the Hill of the Lord!

Rejoice, Meek follower of Christ who inherited the earth;

Rejoice, For you hungered and thirsted for righteousness!

Rejoice, For you showed mercy and received mercy;

Rejoice, Saint, pure of heart, seeing God and His beloved Saints!

Rejoice, Bringer of peace;

Rejoice, You who endured the revilement and insults of men!

Rejoice, Understander of the Wisdom, “The house of the righteous stands firm.”;

Rejoice, For your reward is great in Heaven!

Rejoice, O holy Maximos, Hut-Burner, seer and abider of Heaven!

Kontakion 6

In your earthly ministry you were a constant builder and a rebuilder of ancient ruins, setting your sights on the Heavenly Jerusalem. In God’s image and likeness, with craftsmanship you proclaimed the never ending works of God and enlightened others by the Divine Light bestowed upon you. Wondrous Maximos, of right-glory, even in seclusion you enlightened those who had recourse unto your grace-filled face. Likened to Moses at Mt. Sinai and Jesus Christ at Mt. Tabor, your Divinely-Energized soul and labor moves us, like Peter, James and John to chant unto God: Alleluia!

Ikos 6

The Light of Christ shone forth from you like an all-radiant star, O luminous servant of Christ. God permitted beams of living words to radiate forth from your place of reclusion. He poured out healing springs of grace-filled counsel, slaking the thirst of the little ones and feeding them not earthly stones, but bread like manna from heaven. For this, we who are in shadows and darkness, walk toward the Light of Christ set upon the Holy Mountain with faith, and we cry out to you:

Rejoice, Gentle guide to those that sought God, reminding them they are all sons of God;

Rejoice, Clairvoyant of the paths of men!

Rejoice, Divine traveler witnessed by Igumen Theophanes;

Rejoice, Caller of sinners to repentance from hiding of earthly things!

Rejoice, Godly counselor of Emperors and Patriarchs;

Rejoice, Preparer of Patriarch Kallistos for his unjust repose!

Rejoice, Receiver of bread like manna to feed the hungry;

Rejoice, Giver of water to the thirsty!

Rejoice, Venerated by the Seraphim;

Rejoice, Marveled at by the Cherubim!

Rejoice, Revealer of God to men;

Rejoice, You who worshiped the Word of God in the unobstructed Light by which we see Light!

Rejoice, Unshakeable pillar of right glory!

Rejoice, You who were tireless in the struggles of monastic life;

Rejoice, Strong one far exceeding Samson of old!

Rejoice, Helmsman to the Lighthouse to save our souls from the rock strewn sea;

Rejoice, Learned Builder, who like Cephas, teaches the wise to build their house upon The Rock!

Rejoice, O holy Maximos, Hut-Burner, seer and abider of Heaven!

Kontakion 7

Like Daniel the Prophet, the Man of Godly desires and loved by God, you desired to see the unfading kingdom of the Ancient of Days! O holy Maximos, you spurned the falsehood of the world’s idols and comforts. You found the grace of the Holy Spirit as you replied to “Seek ye My Face”, with “Thy Face I shall seek!” Now we with hope to see God, follow the Light set before us, and chant unto God: Alleluia!

Ikos 7

Perceiving you as one transfigured and a friend of God and the Theotokos, we see one filled and endowed with unwaning Light, full of humility and meekness. People hastened with hope to your abode wherever it may be, trusting in your gracious counsel. We now hasten to your heavenly abode, asking help of you as we cry out such things as these:

Rejoice, Vessel of Grace who sought the gracious Wisdom Who hews the seven pillars of the eternal house;

Rejoice, Abode of the Spirit who inspires in us the desire to abide in Christ and His Father’s House!

Rejoice, Holy Tabernacle partook of the prepared food of immortality, as a doer of the will of Him who sent you;

Rejoice, Abode of Wisdom who says, “Let all who are simple come in here!”

Rejoice, Saint who was enveloped by the Light of Divine Energies;

Rejoice, Spiritual Guide who shows the way to knowledge of God!

Rejoice, Urger of battle against the enemy who desires to entrap us in the snares of despair and decay;

Rejoice, Friend of God who walks the path to the eternal and flees the foreign land of earthly materialism!

Rejoice, Awake Guard who rouses us from sinful sleep of soul-destroying slothfullness;

Rejoice, Radiant presence who illumines our minds which have been darkened by indifference!

Rejoice, Divine Supplicant whose supplications help us kindle the flame in a cold and failing heart;

Rejoice, Grace-Filled One who continually offers supplications unto God for us!

Rejoice, Summit of Virtue who has acquired incorruptible treasure, where neither moth nor rust destroy;

Rejoice, Unceasing intercessor to our Redeemer, praying God grant us time to amend our life!

Rejoice, O holy Maximos, Hut-Burner, seer and abider of Heaven!

Kontakion 8

A strange and wondrous struggle was revealed in your life, O holy Maximos, as one who abided in continual labors and constant thoughts of God, and did acquire an eternal treasure of spiritual understanding. Seeing this, with thanksgiving we chant to God in the compunction of our hearts: Alleluia!

Ikos 8

By the workings of God, you were shown to be a luminary for all men, O holy Maximos. Even to this day, we ponder upon your example to build within our soul that which is eternal, of the Father, by the Son and through the Holy Spirit, one Triune God without confusion. You showed that the rubble and debris of failed buildings and all other obstacles to our soul and our vision of God may be burnt away and removed so that the works of God remain and are made evident. We, the children of God, enlightened by the Light of Christ in you, say unto you with love:

Rejoice, Heavenly servant of the Lord of lords;

Rejoice, Doer of the will of thy Master!

Rejoice, Guide of men upon the path of life;

Rejoice, You who set hearts aflame with love of God our Creator!

Rejoice, Watchful Guard, who encircled the house of your soul with the fear of God;

Rejoice, You who are surrounded with the awareness that God is everywhere present!

Rejoice, Star created by the never-waning Light;

Rejoice, Illuminary who shines forth Light from the Three-Sunned Godhead to illumine us!

Rejoice, Lamp who enlightens benighted souls;

Rejoice, Angel who acquired angelic purity on earth!

Rejoice, Most radiant lamp of piety;

Rejoice, Knower of the Lord as Light and Salvation!

Rejoice, You who knew there to be nothing permanent in this life to abide in or fear;

Rejoice, Confident Warrior for God, though a host encamps against you!

Rejoice, You who were hidden in the secret place of the bosom of Abraham;

Rejoice, You who were set upon the Holy Rock during your life, at your repose and in the Resurrection!

Rejoice, For you did not faint, but believed in the goodness of the Lord in the land of the living;

Rejoice, Reminder of the weak to be of good courage, strengthening our hearts to wait upon the Lord!

Rejoice, O holy Maximos, Hut-Burner, seer and abider of Heaven!

Kontakion 9

In every hour of your transitory life you labored with dynamis as Mary the sister of Lazarus for the one thing needful. Spurning earthly things, you made your soul a pure habitation for the Holy Spirit, and your heart was warmed and nurtured by the constant presence of God, as you chanted unto the Lord: Alleluia!

Ikos 9

Meek and wise, you were shown to all who sought God to be a golden-lampstand with an illumined face like Moses on Sinai and as those on the Mount of Transfiguration. Light and Life accompanied your words to those seeking Divine counsel and fleeing darkness. Wherefore, O blessed Maximos, accept from us such praises as these:

Rejoice, Starets who can look into our passionate souls and guide us to freedom from legion bonds;

Rejoice, Illuminator from God, who sees the mortal shadow of our hearts, wherein is deposited icy coldness

and burning fury!

Rejoice, Teacher that the True Impassible Warmth, Light and Divine Fire may take eternal abode in us;

Rejoice, True Faster who ever reminds us that the Kingdom is more than eating and drinking or corruptible houses!

Rejoice, Temple of the Spirit who helps us uproot from our heart the passionate attachment to the corruptible riches which are heavenly poverty;

Rejoice, Ascetic who teaches us to restrain from idle talking, excess of speech and blasphemy!

Rejoice, Lover of God who renews in our hearts the commandment to love the All-good One and our neighbor as yourself;

Rejoice, Vessel of the Spirit who trains us to glorify our Lord, not only with our mouth but with our heart, mind, strength and soul!

Rejoice, O holy Maximos, Hut-Burner, seer and abider of Heaven!

Kontakion 10

As an abider in Christ, you served as a lampstand to light the way of salvation from darkness to Light for the children of God. O lover of Christ, you related to seekers the mysteries of the invisible warfare and the Light that shines in the darkness. You exemplified the desire of nations in seeking the Ancient of Days. To those who struggle in darkness and thirst for salvation as they desire to abide in the land of the Living One, you taught them to chant in gratitude to God: Alleluia!

Ikos 10

Your divinely wise labors with the Builder and Maker have been shown to be a rampart for the flock of Christ, and a wall of defense against the wolves, wind and waves which would destroy it. O Holy Maximos the Hut-Burner, we your children marvel at the Chief Cornerstone, which has become your and our foundation and we reverently cry out to you thus:

Rejoice, Understanding Heir of Adah’s son Jabal, who lived in tents, prefiguring your nomadic life;

Rejoice, Expounder of the Psalmist and Prophet King when he bespoke “Do good in thy good pleasure unto Zion: build thou the walls of Jerusalem.”!

Rejoice, As those who ascended the Mount as of Transfiguration;

Rejoice, Perceiver with understanding of the Feast of Booths, knowing of a future home for which to say, “It is good for us to be here.”!

Rejoice, Saint illumined by Divine Energies;

Rejoice, Illuminator of souls with the Holy Fire of noetic prayer, like the Holy Fire of Pascha!

Rejoice, You who allowed the Divine One to burn away dross, causing the gold to shine;

Rejoice, You who seeing the purity of the unconsumed Burning Bush, set your vision toward eternal Light!

Rejoice, As Elijah, you were carried aloft on a fiery chariot of prayer to the heavens;

Rejoice, Passer of a mantle of prayer to the watchful that gaze in prayer to the heavens!

Rejoice, Burner of obstacles to your ascent, that you may proceed to the Holy Mountain;

Rejoice, True inhabitant of the City of God, set on a hill, that many seek!

Rejoice, O holy Maximos, Hut-Burner, seer and abider of Heaven!

Kontakion 11

O wondrous luminary and craftsman trained by God! Building upon a solid Rock, supernaturally illumined by the Divine Energies of God, in Wisdom you pointed the flock to the Gladsome Light! Never cease to mediate for us before our Lord in heaven, where you stand in constant glorification with a great cloud of witnesses, crying out to Him in radiant splendor with all the Saints, Cherubim, Seraphim, thrones, dominions, principalities, powers and all the angelic hosts of Heaven: Alleluia!

Ikos 11

Your splendid life was not hidden from those who sought the righteousness of God. You were set upon a Holy Mountain, and were not obscured by any obstacle, O all-wondrous lover of God! For your sacrifices and peaceful repose we give thanks to God. With our minds resting within our hearts and souls, and with our lips we say with compunction:

Rejoice, Saint who reminds us to count the cost of building before we embark upon the effort;

Rejoice, Rebuilder of the ancient ruins!

Rejoice, Understander of, “Is this not the fast I have chosen?”

Rejoice, Exemplar of undoing of burdens to free the oppressed and in breaking of yokes!

Rejoice, Reprover of the slothful, despairing, and idle talkers;

Rejoice, Servant with chastity, humility and patience!

Rejoice, Inspirer of us to always utter with our tongue only those things which are needful and profitable;

Rejoice, Blessed vessel of purity of body and soul!

Rejoice, Servant of the Lord to the end of your temporal hut;

Rejoice, Saint, who after your repose, was recognized as worthy of everlasting blessedness!

Rejoice, Wondrous ornament of Athos;

Rejoice, Inheritor and eternal resident in the kingdom of God!

Rejoice, O holy Maximos, Hut-Burner, seer and abider of Heaven!

Kontakion 12

We offer you laudations and hymns, O Maximos the Hut-Burner, as we seek to bless you who by your life and struggles glorified the Holy name of our Creator and God. We pray our labors be not in vain, and that we may follow Christ’s example in you. By your supplications vouchsafe that we also may lead a life of right glory, that with you we may ever chant unto God: Alleluia!

Ikos 12

Hymning your ascetic labors as we wonder at the path of ascent of the Saints to the abode of the Father, Son and Holy Spirit, we fall down with hope before your precious icon and relics, O holy Maximos the Hut-Burner, Seer and Friend of God. We beseech you as a heavenly protector and great intercessor for us before the throne of the Lord of glory, as we cry out with humility:

Rejoice, Divine Seeker of the eternal Father’s House;

Rejoice, Saint who prepared at all times for eternal repose!

Rejoice, Wondrous ascetic of God;

Rejoice, Mighty intercessor for those who trust in you!

Rejoice, Visionary who teaches us to look upon every day as the last of our life;

Rejoice, Mighty Laborer whose strength was renewed like the eagles, to take flight to the heavens!

Rejoice, Sanctified One who endured to the end that you may be saved;

Rejoice, Saintly Prophet, who like Elijah’s passing his mantle to Elisha, bestowed a blessed hut to Niphon!

Rejoice, Temple of the Spirit, whose grave was prepared by God;

Rejoice, New Wine Skin, who shed the old soil of Adam for eternal union in the flesh of Christ, the new Adam!

Rejoice, Abode of the Spirit, who by Divine Grace exceeded the three score and ten by a score and five;

Rejoice, Saint who reposed near the Lavra of Saint Athanasius!

Rejoice, Vessel of Grace whose reposed body emitted the aroma of herbs and fragrant incense;

Rejoice, Saint still blooming as a most fragrant luminous flower for all time!

Rejoice, Traveler in the Holy Ark across land and sea to the eternal land;

Rejoice, Peer of Angels, who dwells with the great cloud of witnesses!

Rejoice, Divine Intercessor who prays unceasingly for the Kingdom to come;

Rejoice, Most excellent luminary with all the luminaries in the House of God!

Rejoice, Good Friend of Christ, beloved of God;

Rejoice, Guest at the Divine Table of the Lord!

Rejoice, Fervent mediator of salvation for our souls;

Rejoice, Comforter amid our tribulations of building that which will endure!

Kontakion 13

(read three times)

Pray we may see the True Light and be radiant when the stone is rolled away from the tomb covering our souls, and that the old garment may be laid aside. Pray that the Gladsome Light of the Holy, Immortal One shine upon and within us. Help us tear down all obstacles and build rightly within our souls. Pray that we may aid one another, as God builds the house. Accept from us this meager entreaty and our grateful praise, which we offer unto you from the depths of our souls. Forget not us sinners in your supplications. We trust in God and the revelation of His work in you, O Hut-Burner. May we unceasingly cry out to the Lord of hosts with all the choir of Heaven: Alleluia! Alleluia! Alleluia!

Kontakion 1 (repeated)

O luminary of the Church, chosen by the Lord in your youth, blessed Hut-Burner Maximos, a Divine visionary and inspiring ascetic and humble servant of Jesus Christ! We praise you with love and honor for the struggles and labors you showed forth in seeking of the Heavenly Kingdom. You took not refuge in the house of man, but as Abraham you sought the City Whose Builder and Maker is God. Wandering and abiding in seclusion, you tore down all obstacles that obscured your vision of God and Heaven and traced a path of salvation with a Divinely-Energized life. As you now stand before the throne of God, pray for us who cry to you:

Rejoice, O holy Maximos, Hut-Burner, seer and abider of Heaven!

Prayer to St. Maximos

Divine laborer Maximos, we hymn you with voices of praise. Pray that God grant us the Light of grace from His providence on high, so that we may flee from the darkness of the passions to eternal Light and sing fervently the joyful story of your life.

Like Abraham you sought the City of God and the Mountain of the Most High. Through your self-sacrifice you became a righteous friend of God and a light that may not be hid upon the Holy Mountain. You labored not in vain, but with God Who built an eternal house. Pray that we too, with the Grace of God, may burn away all obstructions and build that which endures and is deified by Christ.

Exemplified by the Burning Bush, you were enflamed but not consumed by the love of God. Like you and the Magi and all the Saints, may we set our eyes upon the pre-eternal Light. We join you and St. Symeon the Metaphrastes who said of God, “You are a fire, and You consume the unworthy! Burn me not, O my Maker, But rather pass through my composite members into all my joints, my innermost core, and my heart.” If our huts and works be burnt up like stubble and grass, pray that we be not consumed by other than the love of God and that we shall be saved and purified like gold in the furnace by the Holy One.

Please pray to the Lord for us as we cry out that He will purify us before He raises us from this place. Blessed Saint, you taught that verily everyone shall return and disappear in his mother the earth, and shall be dissolved at once, to receive either honors or punishments as reward for what he has done in his lifetime. Pray that Christ God sanctifies our souls, purifies our bodies, sets our minds aright, cleanses our thoughts and delivers us from all sorrow, evil, and distress, that surrounded by His holy angels and guarded and guided by their host, we may attain to the unity of the faith and an understanding of His ineffable glory.

Following your holy example, we petition the Father of Lights for an unhindered and undistracted ascent on the ladder to eternal residence in His Kingdom and House. Pray God provides us with your wisdom and piety, that we too may see Heaven declaring the Glory of God and be counted among the host glorifying God: Father, Son and Holy Spirit, the Holy Trinity in One Essence!

Amen.
