Akathist to St. Tikhon, Patriarch of Moscow

Served on the feasts of our Holy Father Tikhon, Patriarch of Moscow (April 7 and October 9) and whenever appointed.

The Opening

Priest: Blessed is our God, always, now and ever and unto ages of ages.

Reader: Amen Glory to Thee O God. Glory to Thee. O Heavenly King, the Comforter, the Spirit of Truth, Who art everywhere and fills all things. Treasury of blessings and giver of life, come and abide in us, and cleanse us from every impurity. And, save our souls, O Good One.

Reader: Holy God, Holy Mighty , Holy Immortal, have mercy on us (3).

Reader: Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever and unto ages of ages. Amen.

Reader: O Most Holy Trinity, have mercy on us. Lord, cleanse us from our sins. Master, pardon our transgressions. Holy One visit and heal our infirmities for Thy name's sake.

Reader: Lord, have mercy (3).

Reader: Glory to the Father, and to the Son and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever and unto ages of ages. Amen.

Reader: Our Father, Who art in heaven. Hallowed be Thy name. Thy kingdom come. Thy will be done, on earth, as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread and forgive us our trespasses as we forgive those who trespass against us. And, lead us not into temptation but deliver us from evil.

Priest: For thine is the kingdom and the power and the glory of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, now and ever and unto ages of ages.

Reader: Amen.

Kontakion 1

Choir/Faithful: O Chosen Hierarch, most glorious favorite and confessor of Christ, strong defender of the Orthodox Church, intercessor and supplicant for both Russia and America, who glorified the Lord who glorified you by granting us such a great and new and glorious witness, we sing this hymn of praise to you; and as you have great boldness before the Queen of Heaven and earth, deliver us from all calamity who call to you and sing;

Rejoice, O Holy Hierarch Tikhon, the pride and confirmation of the Orthodox Church.

Ikos 1

Priest: Desiring to comfort the people suffering through the turbulent and stormy days of revolution, the Lord and Creator of the Angels gave them you, O Most serene and kind hearted Patriarch, to be a beacon illuminating the souls of the faithful with the radiance of the Thrice Luminous Trinity; and even now, all those wanting to be with Christ follow in your steps. Therefore we humbly cry:

Rejoice, Obedient monastic who accepted the patriarch's throne.

Rejoice, Zealous fulfillment of the will of God.

Rejoice, All-holy Master who ascended the throne on the Feast of the Virgin.

Rejoice, O Good and faithful servant who was announced before the throne of the Trinity on the Feast of Her Annunciation.

Rejoice, Confessor adorned in the robes of a patriarch.

Rejoice, Luminary who dedicated his life to God and was illumined by Him.

Rejoice, Confirmation of the Patriarchate of the Russian Church.

Rejoice, O Blessed icon of the Good Shepherd.

Rejoice, Magnificent adornment of the Donskoi Monastery.

Rejoice, Confirmation of the Church in America.

Rejoice, Divinely ordained inheritance of the Russian lands.

Rejoice, Divinely inspired instructor of the Orthodox in America.

Rejoice, O Holy Hierarch Tikhon, the pride and confirmation of the Orthodox Church.

Kontakion 2

Priest: Seeing the Russian people afraid and in great despair over the desecration of their Orthodox churches, you preferred they should seek eternal life through prayer and supplication rather than through earthly death. Enlighten us also, O Tender-hearted Tikhon, with the light of your wisdom that together we may sing to the Creator of all: Alleluia.

Choir/Faithful: Alleluia, Alleluia, Alleluia.

Ikos 2

Priest: With wisdom inspired by God, O Godly wise Tikhon, you denied the wisdom of the flesh, and wisely dedicated your will to the Lord. Offering yourself as a pure receptacle you became an abode of the Holy Spirit. Therefore we praise you as a godly wise teacher and we sing:

Rejoice, Great elder, for the patriarchal throne was foretold to you in the flower of your youth.

Rejoice, Chosen laborer in the vineyard of Christ.

Rejoice, Enlightened pastor and nourisher of the Orthodox Church in America.

Rejoice, Good shepherd whose flock followed thee to the heights of spiritual deeds.

Rejoice, Good pastor who lead his flock to salvation.

Rejoice, Champion of the Kingdom on High.

Rejoice, Visionary and preacher of the Wisdom of God.

Rejoice, Guardian of the wisdom of the Apostolic Church.

Rejoice, Golden-mouthed herald of Orthodox truth.

Rejoice, Fearless exposer of lies and deceit.

Rejoice, Most wonderful fragrance of righteousness.

Rejoice, O Master who has not put our hopes to shame.

Rejoice, O Holy Hierarch Tikhon, the pride and confirmation of the Orthodox Church.

Kontakion 3

Priest: The power of God was made manifest in your Cross-bearing labors, O Preacher and Hierarch Tikhon, for being armed with the grace of God you destroyed the evil works of the countless enemies of Orthodoxy and sang to God the song of victory: Alleluia.

Choir/Faithful: Alleluia, Alleluia, Alleluia.

Ikos 3

Priest: Having an unshakable faith and a conscience clear you forsook the comforts of this world, O Tikhon, blessed of God; and as a Hierarch of the Church, you labored mightily for the Lord. Therefore, being adorned with blessedness and being illumined from on-High, as the North Star you guide us all who sing:

Rejoice, Example of meekness who loved Christ with your whole heart.

Rejoice, Wise servant who increased the talent entrusted to you.

Rejoice, O Virtuous one who accomplished things pleasing to God.

Rejoice, Invincible warrior who armed yourself with wisdom, humility and piety.

Rejoice, Master who confounded the godless with holiness.

Rejoice, O Master who sought after righteousness and truth.

Rejoice, O Patriarch who boldly proclaimed the truth.

Rejoice, O Servant who suffered imprisonment for the sake of the Truth.

Rejoice, O Monk who attained the heights through humility.

Rejoice, Great ascetic who achieved the eternal reward through your spiritual deeds.

Rejoice, Good shepherd who steadfastly ensured the safety of your flock.

Rejoice, O Righteous one who fervently entreats God for our sake.

Rejoice, O Holy Hierarch Tikhon, the pride and confirmation of the Orthodox Church.

Kontakion 4

Priest: The raging storms of our passion filled thoughts do not allow us, O Most wise Tikhon, to properly extol the magnitude of your witness and confession, yet being strengthened in faith and piety through your prayers before the Throne of the Almighty, we fervently sing to Him: Alleluia.

Choir/Faithful: Alleluia, Alleluia, Alleluia.

Ikos 4

Priest: When the faithful learned of the beneficent goodness of your soul, O God-bearing Tikhon, they were filled with a yearning desire to see you ascend to the patriarchal throne. Since you were chosen from on-High to be the instrument of Divine command, we are filled with wonder at the providence of God and we sing these songs of praise:

Rejoice, Chief Hierarch elected by God.

Rejoice, Chosen man selected to lead the flock of Christ.

Rejoice, Divinely-chosen of the High Priest Christ Himself.

Rejoice, Confirmation of the eternal Christian ideals.

Rejoice, Communicant together with the Apostles.

Rejoice, Concelebrant with the great Hierarchs of the Church.

Rejoice, Godly-wise equal to the Patriarchs of old.

Rejoice, Proven guide on the path of salvation.

Rejoice, Vanquisher of the logic of the flesh.

Rejoice, Ceaseless proclamation of salvific grace.

Rejoice, Ever-flowing source of miracles.

Rejoice, Supplicant for the people, starets and favorite of Rus'.

Rejoice, O Holy Hierarch Tikhon, the pride and confirmation of the Orthodox Church.

Kontakion 5

Priest: The Lord revealed you as a divinely-bright star, O Most-wonderful Father Tikhon, and you shine upon your flock with the radiant light of Orthodoxy, revealing to us the steps which lead to the Kingdom of Heaven and teaching us to ever sing to God: Alleluia.

Choir/Faithful: Alleluia, Alleluia, Alleluia.

Ikos 5

Priest: When you ascended to the throne of patriarchal primacy, vacant for two hundred years, you gave yourself completely to the Lord. You labored mightily for God illuminating those darkened by their passions. Seeing you, therefore, as a godly-wise Archpastor and a most faithful and able builder of the House of God we sing this hymn of praise:

Rejoice, Firm pilot standing at the helm of the Church.

Rejoice, Bright beacon of Christ to the faithful.

Rejoice, Provider of solace to those needing spiritual comfort.

Rejoice, Guardian of the Church against the hour of your death.

Rejoice, Brave pastor who kept the devourer of souls from devouring your flock.

Rejoice, True helmsman who preserved the Church of Christ in times of calamity.

Rejoice, Great leader who delivered us from the prince of this world.

Rejoice, Wise teacher who filled us with desire for Christ.

Rejoice, Beauty of theology and beauty in deeds.

Rejoice, Wisdom that confounded the fury of persecution.

Rejoice, Strength that stemmed the temptations of the flesh.

Rejoice, Pride of the Orthodox faith.

Rejoice, O Holy Hierarch Tikhon, the pride and confirmation of the Orthodox Church.

Kontakion 6

Priest: Truly you appeared as a preacher of grace, a bold defense and a righteous proclamation of the Word of Truth, O Blessed Hierarch Tikhon, for as a true soldier of Christ, you defended your flock during the time of mortal sin and temptation, calling all to sing to the One True God: Alleluia.

Choir/Faithful: Alleluia, Alleluia, Alleluia.

Ikos 6

Priest: Having labored in the New World and having planted the foundations of monastic life in America, you ascended the patriarchal throne of Russia as a radiant star shimmering with the rays of your teachings and dispelling the darkness of evil with the light of your confession. Now we satisfy ourselves with the fruit of your labors and with thankfulness and love we sing:

Rejoice, O Hierarch who ascended the throne of primacy through the providence of God.

Rejoice, True pastor who followed the way of the Cross together with the flock entrusted to you.

Rejoice, Loving father who lead us with your shepherd's staff.

Rejoice, O Master who did not abandon us in the wilderness.

Rejoice, Good shepherd who returned the lost sheep who was stolen away.

Rejoice, Physician who cared for the wounded and healed the sick.

Rejoice, Vigilant leader who separated the rebellious and the violent from the Lambs of your flock.

Rejoice, Confessor who stanched the flow of heresies and malicious slander.

Rejoice, Instructor of the faithful who lamented strife in the Church.

Rejoice, O High priest who withstood false accusations against your monastic life.

Rejoice, Precious treasure of the Church who preserved the Orthodox Faith as a treasure for all people.

Rejoice, Good worker in the vineyard whose labors still show us the way today.

Rejoice, O Holy Hierarch Tikhon, the pride and confirmation of the Orthodox Church.

Kontakion 7

Priest: Desiring ever to be a good shepherd to your flock, O God-bearing Tikhon, you strove to achieve all things for the benefit of their souls, so that they might come to the One True God. Therefore we, who desire to achieve salvation through your most glorious intercessions, dare to sing: Alleluia.

Choir/Faithful: Alleluia, Alleluia, Alleluia.

Ikos 7

Priest: The Church in both Russia and America has found in you, O Holy Hierarch Tikhon, a new and glorious patron, for through your confession of faith you withstood the assaults of men while not forsaking the commands of the Holy Fathers. Therefore, you were given spiritual strength by the Author of spiritual perfection, Christ Himself and we, the unworthy sing:

Rejoice, Example of abstinence.

Rejoice, Source of salvation for countless souls.

Rejoice, Shepherd who drove the wolves away from your sheep.

Rejoice, Father who wisely exposed their treacherous ways.

Rejoice, Champion who defeated satanic assaults.

Rejoice, Warrior who wielded the all-powerful weapon of grace.

Rejoice, Herald of God's providence for man.

Rejoice, Wonderful purification of morality.

Rejoice, Vigilant guardian of the treasures of the Orthodox Faith.

Rejoice, Favorite of God who tasted the pleasures of Heaven while abiding here on earth.

Rejoice, Revelation of the sweetness of your soul with the deeds of your life.

Rejoice, O Holy Hierarch Tikhon, the pride and confirmation of the Orthodox Church.

Kontakion 8

Priest: The unbelievers and the weak in faith beheld a strange and glorious miracle, O God-pleasing Tikhon, when they witnessed your blessed life on the patriarchal throne, for in the face of evil oppression you confirmed the Orthodox Faith and through your wisdom and works of grace, you taught us, your faithful flock to piously sing: Alleluia.

Choir/Faithful: Alleluia, Alleluia, Alleluia

Ikos 8

Rejoice, Great Master who astonished the world with your love.

Rejoice, Example of meekness in authority.

Prayer to Holy Hierarch Tikhon

Priest: O most-gracious pastor, great and holy Patriarch Tikhon. You appeared as a fortress standing high atop a hill, and your many good deeds shine forth before us even to this day. And now, standing before the Throne of the Most-Holy Trinity, you pray ceaselessly with great boldness to the Lord and Creator of all. Look down upon your sinful and unworthy children who fall down before you in earnest prayer and entreat the Lord that He may grant us the determination to firmly strive in the faith of our fathers as you firmly strove from the days of your youth, and having been an ardent defender and protector of the true Faith during this life, help us to preserve the Orthodox Faith. Seeing before us the calm and serene example of your peaceful soul, enable us to humbly accept the will of God rather than be concerned with the tempestuous wisdom of man. Bravely you stood before the enemies of Christ, boldly proclaiming the One True God, strengthen us weaklings therefore with your prayers, that we too may in every place and at every time stand against the forces of atheism and lies. O champion of Christ, do not despise us as we bring our prayers to you, for not only are we asking for deliverance from our sorrows and misfortunes, we are also asking for strength and determination, and the spirit of love and grace, that we might overcome all the perils that befall us. Pray that we may be granted the required patience to reach the end of our days, that we may attain the peace of our Lord and that we may receive forgiveness of our sins. O holy father Tikhon, disperse the winds of strife and faithlessness, so that our Lord may establish the peaceful calm of Faith and uncompromising Love in all the Russian and American lands, that civil strife and discord may be abated through your prayers, and that our Holy Orthodox Church may flourish, never wanting for honest workers and true pastors to rightfully divide the Word of Thy Truth. Protect and preserve the flock of Christ, and ceaselessly entreat the Lord of Hosts that our lands will embrace the spirit of repentance, that we may all with one mind and one heart ever praise God, Who is wonderful in His saints, the Trinity, glorified in the Father, and the Son and the Holy Spirit, always, now and ever and unto ages of ages.

Choir/Faithful: Amen.
