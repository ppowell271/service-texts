Akathist to Saint Macarius the Great

Served on the feast of our Venerable Father Macarius the Great of Egypt (January 19) and whenever appointed.

The text begins with Kontakion 1; the usual opening (Blessed is our God, O Heavenly King, the Trisagion prayers, “God Is the Lord” and the troparion) is not printed in it. The file also contains a canon to St. Macarius, a second akathist (in Romanian, with a machine translation), a life from the Prologue of Ohrid, and a synaxarion, which are not included here.

Kontakion 1

Blessed life in the world, you were worthy to dwell in the land of the meek, with the company of the faithful, Macarius, bearer of God, and dwelling in the wilderness as in a city, you received from Christ the grace of miracles. For this reason we honor you:

Rejoice, Holy Venerable Macarius, Father of fathers!

Ikos 1

From Egypt and of a name with the Holy ancestors Abraham and Sarah, your parents embellished their lives with temperance and fasting, with prayers and vigils, with almsgiving and many other good deeds. Nonetheless, they were still united in spirit. For this reason the whole ray of the love of the Holy Trinity they lodged in their hearts, and the Holy Patriarch Abraham himself proclaimed to them in a vision the unbinding of their darkness by the joyful fruit which was to be the light of the worlds:

Rejoice, with your father the priest Abraham, in the joy of the vision of the holy patriarch;

Rejoice, that your parents, having had their wealth taken from them, hoped in the Lord;

Rejoice, for they were comforted by the divine light of good news;

Rejoice, for they did not delay to follow the will of the Lord;

Rejoice, for they have moved according to God’s ordinance near the wilderness where you were to serve Him;

Rejoice, that your father was raised from the deadly disease with a steady hand;

Rejoice, for the angel of God has proclaimed to him mercy and blessings from above;

Rejoice, that the Holy Ghost has you as His abode;

Rejoice, for the name of happiness He has given you;

Rejoice, that the angelic life, fruitful of the salvation of many, has painted you;

Rejoice, for your parents gave thanks to the Lord with joy and trembling;

Rejoice, holy Macarius, father of fathers!

Kontakion 2

Begotten by your parents in their old age, you ran, happy, with godly longing, to the fountain of the knowledge of God. And by having learned the Holy Scriptures, you tirelessly walked on the paths of wisdom, and in your heart kept the light of the holy hymn of: Alleluia!

Ikos 2

With the blessed life, as with an untainted bride, you desired. Your parents, forgetting the angelic counsel, compelled you to be joined with a woman, and you soared like a bird above the hunters’ trap. Thus you were able to fulfill your parents’ will and to keep untouched the virginity of body and soul, which you dedicated to God:

Rejoice, for in distress you have lifted up the eyes of your soul to God;

Rejoice, for He has put good counsel in your heart;

Rejoice, for you have done His will with steadfast hope;

Rejoice, feigning sickness, after the wedding feast you went out and you ran to the help above.

Rejoice, for so you have kept your virginity to the Lord;

Rejoice, you went to Nitria and in a dream you saw a wonderful man, shining like light;

Rejoice, he showed you the wilderness where you were to dwell;

Rejoice, when you returned home, your bride went to the Lord in virginity;

Rejoice, you humbled yourself by the thought of death;

Rejoice, denying earthly things and entrusting yourself to God;

Rejoice, that your parents also rejoiced in the purity of your life;

Rejoice, that you served them with love and devotion until death;

Rejoice, holy Macarius, father of fathers!

Kontakion 3

You sought, blessed Macarius, with zeal, a holy guide on the way of salvation and you always prayed to God for it. On the feast of one of the saints, when you were preparing meals, you saw a monk in the church, who had come to receive the holy sacraments, and you knew the face of the blessed life. For this reason you called him to the feast after the Liturgy, taking him aside, you asked him to be your good advisor in life, so that together you might sing to God, Alleluia!

Ikos 3

The next day you ran to the old monk, who received your confession and the secrets of the heart, after which he taught you for a long time. And in the evening, while you were resting, he lifted up his mind in prayer to God and saw in vision a choir of monks with white faces, and wings, who urged you to divine awakening and zeal in the service of God:

Rejoice, for through your teacher Christ has called you to the salvation of many;

Rejoice, that through him you have received the angelic calling;

Rejoice, for he hastened you to the pleasing of the Lord;

Rejoice, for he has taught you much about prayer, fasting and watchfulness;

Rejoice, returning home, you shared all that you had with the poor;

Rejoice, that you embraced poverty willingly and followed Christ;

Rejoice, that in the hands of the holy old man you have forsaken your will;

Rejoice, that he received it and served it with love before God;

Rejoice, for he taught you the work of your hands to the spiritually minded;

Rejoice, building a cell nearby, he spiritually edified you;

Rejoice, for he begat you to blessed angelic life;

Rejoice, holy Macarius, father of fathers!

Kontakion 4

You increased, Holy Macarius, in the law of the Lord, by running on the narrow path of the monastic life, so much so that the bishop of the city, learning of your happy life, made you a cleric with all his heart, though you were still young. But you fled, for you were longing for the peace of God, in whom you continually sang to Him: Alleluia!

Ikos 4

By the work of your hands and unceasing prayer of your heart you have strengthened yourself, blessed one, by fencing yourself with the Cross of the Lord in the war of thoughts, delusions and fearful rages which the devil has made war upon you through men, making a young woman who had been born of fornication cast the most terrible sin upon you:

Rejoice, unceasing watchfulness of spirit;

Rejoice, you are free from the troubles of the world;

Rejoice, you have been unjustly blamed and reproached;

Rejoice, for you have been dragged through the village with black scorn;

Rejoice, for you have been pulled by the hair and trampled under foot;

Rejoice, that you were beaten with sticks by all to near death;

Rejoice, for they barely left you alive, and made an honest man your steward;

Rejoice, that he was ready to die for you and brought you to his cell;

Rejoice, that you worked day and night to feed the woman with child;

Rejoice, she did not give birth until she confessed her adultery;

Rejoice, they all repented, and ran to ask your forgiveness;

Rejoice, you fled into the wilderness;

Rejoice, holy Macarius, father of fathers!

Kontakion 5

Three years you spent in a cave, Father, after which you went to Saint Anthony the Great on Mount Paran, and he lovingly received you as his disciple in the hermitage. And you increased by obedience, so that you were called by the other brothers an old young elder. For from your youth you showed a chosen life and fought day and night against the devils, steadfastly guarding the wall founded by Christ, to whom you sang: Alleluia!

Ikos 5

By humility and unceasing remembrance of God you quenched the multitude of the enemy’s arrows, taking from Christ, forty years after your birth, the gift of healing and prophecy, as well as dominion over unclean spirits. Then you were given the priesthood and guided the fathers of those living in the hermitage:

Rejoice, O soul possessed by the fire of the Spirit;

Rejoice, heart strengthened with spiritual nourishment;

Rejoice, sanctified church where Christ dwells;

Rejoice, for you have often sought out the great Anthony;

Rejoice, for you have been greatly taught by him;

Rejoice, together you have shared the strength of spiritual words;

Rejoice, you have inherited the rod on which St. Anthony supported his body;

Rejoice, that like Elisha from Elijah you took and doubled your spirit;

Rejoice, for you have cast out the devilish princes with fasting and prayer;

Rejoice, that by the pouring of holy water you delivered a woman from the likeness of an animal;

Rejoice, that you then led her to greater zeal in the sacraments;

Rejoice, for you have dispelled the deceitfulness of those who are bewitched;

Rejoice, holy Macarius, father of fathers!

Kontakion 6

By anointing a maiden with holy oil you made her healthy, and by prayer you healed many from sicknesses, spells and heretical delusions, even the dead you raised up by the power of Christ, so that in His glory the most holy song may rise from many: Alleluia!

Ikos 6

You drove out of the one who was possessed by the devil, who worked through him false miracles and heretical imaginations. By the bishop’s earnest request you prayed from the depths of your heart and for the salvation of the many deceived you armed yourself with faith and raised a dead man. The devil was then driven out with power, and you baptized the risen one, giving to Christ a multitude of people:

Rejoice, through whom the glory of God has been revealed;

Rejoice, overcoming the power of the enemy by the work of the Holy Spirit;

Rejoice, you have received the divine inspiration;

Rejoice, holy constancy, lest you fall into vain glory;

Rejoice, that through you Christ has wiped away the tears of the afflicted;

Rejoice, for you have revealed to the grieving widow her hidden refuge;

Rejoice, for you raised up her husband to tell where he had laid up his foreign treasure;

Rejoice, deliverer of widows and orphans from bitter bondage;

Rejoice, you raised up a dead man, that you may deliver him who was wrongfully accused;

Rejoice, that the sleeping man has confessed his innocence in the grave;

Rejoice, that you sought not to find him guilty, hoping in the judgment of God;

Rejoice, that you showed then that your work was mercy and not judgment;

Rejoice, holy Macarius, father of fathers!

Kontakion 7

You healed the obstinate one, who had the devil of drunkenness in him and eating all the food he received like nothing, by prayer and fasting, led him to temperance and working with his hands. And in the Holy Spirit you prayed for everyone, so that even those in the toils of hell you were filled with mercy. For this we sing with you to God: Alleluia!

Ikos 7

By the power of God you forced the devil to show the multitude of tricks with which he tempted the monks of the monastery, and the only one of the brothers who did his will, you brought to pure confession and to increase in the good fight:

Rejoice, to overcome his shame you showed yourself to the fallen one yourself tempted by the passions that possessed him;

Rejoice, for so he found boldness to confess and repent;

Rejoice, for you have strengthened him in fasting and in the mind of God;

Rejoice, for you have encouraged him to learn parts of the Gospel and other holy books;

Rejoice, for you taught him not to entertain evil thoughts;

Rejoice, that the brother then showed himself more opposed to evil than all others;

Rejoice, you having received two rich young men and found them to be great followers of Christ;

Rejoice, that through toil and patience they were filled with God’s grace;

Rejoice, that you saw the angel of God guarding them with a sword of fire;

Rejoice, that it was revealed to you how fire went out of their mouths to heaven;

Rejoice, that, as they slept in the Lord, you strengthened the brethren with the revelation of their holiness;

Rejoice, all-seeing virtue;

Rejoice, holy Macarius, father of fathers!

Kontakion 8

The pure and blameless life of the two married women was revealed to you by the Most High, so that you yourself marveled and said “Truly neither virgins nor married women, neither monk nor groom, but the good decision God seeks, receiving it as the deed itself, and by choice sends the Holy Spirit to each one, who works and directs the lives of those who want to be saved. For this reason, in humility of heart we keep the most holy hymn: Alleluia!

Ikos 8

Together with Saint Macarius of Alexandria, your friend in the Lord, you were exiled by Bishop Lucius, an Arian heretic, who banished the blessed Bishop Peter. And led by soldiers into an islet where the name of Christ God was unknown, you drove out the devils with prayer and the working of miracles. Then the idol-worshiping people received faith in Christ and were baptized, building a holy church to the glory of God:

Rejoice, fortress strengthened by spiritual brotherly love;

Rejoice, deliverance of the idolatrous servant’s daughter from evil;

Rejoice, apostolic worthiness;

Rejoice, that you have begotten many to life in Christ;

Rejoice, that the proclamation of your wonders has gone forth to all the earth;

Rejoice, shame of the wicked and unbelieving persecutors;

Rejoice, that the heretic bishop has sent in secret to return you to the wilderness;

Rejoice, that a multitude of people came to you;

Rejoice, you taught many by words of God;

Rejoice, healer of infirmities of soul and body;

Rejoice, resting place of the strangers;

Rejoice, gentle rebuke of those overcome by iniquity;

Rejoice, holy Macarius, father of fathers!

Kontakion 9

The silver-loving disciple, who disregarded your rebuke and did not cleanse himself from the leprosy of passion, after your death, the leprosy of the flesh took hold of him, and the fearful idolatrous servant you have made, a lamb of Christ. For this we praise the mercy and justice with which God takes care of His creatures, and we run to the unbroken shore of love of the Holy Trinity, singing with you: Alleluia!

Ikos 9

You struggled for five years with the thought of going into the wilderness, fearing the temptation of the enemy. But when the will of the Lord has revealed to you through your long patience, you went there and found two monks who for thirty years had been destitute in the heat and frost with divine strength. For which, Father, you sank still further into the ocean of humility, deepening your mind in the pure fear of the Lord. For by remembrance of the judgment and the eternal fire of iniquity, you melted your body as with fierce whips:

Rejoice, for you have died to the world, but have found life in Christ;

Rejoice, you untouched by the arrows of the devil;

Rejoice, you are untainted by the venom of praise;

Rejoice, perfect freedom from the worldly things;

Rejoice, lowliness of self to the glory of Christ;

Rejoice, silent dwelling place of the divine Word;

Rejoice, falling prostrate before the saving face;

Rejoice, lifting up your hands to receive divine grace;

Rejoice, fleeing from men to be joined to God;

Rejoice, call of mercy from on high, which stays the war of the passions;

Rejoice, that you have received in your heart the imprint of love;

Rejoice, flight of worship to the eternity of Love.

Rejoice, holy Macarius, father of fathers!

Kontakion 10

As a joyful partaker of the unbridled joy of the Resurrection you showed yourself, Father, for you covered human weaknesses and labored in prayer for the deliverance of the tempted, bringing forth to the despairing world the strength of the angelic song: Alleluia!

Ikos 10

The robber who stole from your cell, you helped as a stranger, and the disciple tempted by a harlot, ran in prayer to your intercession, an unseen hand took him away, bringing him into the cell, where you prayed to God for him. For this, we joyfully sing to you:

Rejoice, stranger from all transitory things;

Rejoice, you who are planted in heavenly realities;

Rejoice, beholding the world as through the eyes of the icon;

Rejoice, painting on the tablet of the heart the icon of Love;

Rejoice, glory and thanksgiving to God for all;

Rejoice, fountain of heavenly peace;

Rejoice, foretaste of those far and near;

Rejoice, soul cleansed to the depths of God’s grace;

Rejoice, strength of love that sustains the tempted;

Rejoice, you who raise the fallen;

Rejoice, sharer of Christ’s comfort in suffering;

Rejoice, you who have been caught up through the air by the Divine Hand;

Rejoice, holy Macarius, father of fathers!

Kontakion 11

After ninety-seven years of living in Christ, your blessed end was proclaimed to you in a vision by St. Anthony, together with St. Pachomius. And of the approaching call to dwell with them in the Bosom of the Heavenly Father you joyfully received from them the glorious peace of Christ and sang: Alleluia!

Ikos 11

Blessed one, you called the disciples, entrusting them to the Lord, exhorting them to keep the spiritual establishments and the teachings of the monastery, after which you have carefully ordered the assembly. Then you laid your hands on them, blessed them, and prayed for them, teaching them, and prepared yourself for the great and fearful crossing of the threshold of eternity:

Rejoice, flower of the wilderness, refreshed by the Holy Spirit;

Rejoice, for after three days, you went to the Lord;

Rejoice, for a cherub with a host of angels has greeted you;

Rejoice, that you were accompanied by angelic and holy hosts;

Rejoice, you treasure of the King, ascended into the eternal treasuries;

Rejoice, that by your last kiss you left the world;

Rejoice, eyes and hands outstretched towards the Lord to the end;

Rejoice, for into His arms you have given your spirit;

Rejoice, that your soul has been seen ascending to heaven unaffected by the enemy;

Rejoice, for you have covered yourself with humility even within the gates of the kingdom;

Rejoice, you fire that burns all the enemy’s devices;

Rejoice, you who are confined by the power of Christ;

Rejoice, holy Macarius, father of fathers!

Kontakion 12

The seal of the image of Christ and of the Spirit you received, blessed, uniting through love your nature with Divine Grace. For this, at the wedding of the Son of God, you have deigned to sing with the heavenly Church: Alleluia!

Ikos 12

The art that leads to heaven, Father, you have mastered, and you made yourself a new foundation for the enlightenment and strengthening of those who seek your intercession in faith. For this we sing to you:

Rejoice, son of Light and comfort;

Rejoice, son of the New Covenant;

Rejoice, that by the difficult path you have come to the blessed life;

Rejoice, life made an icon of everlasting devotion;

Rejoice, steadfast in love and truth;

Rejoice, eternal union with Christ;

Rejoice, repose in the embrace of the Holy Spirit;

Rejoice, delight in the bosom of the Father of lights;

Rejoice, life-giving witness to the Cross and the Resurrection of the Lord;

Rejoice, living sacrifice, well pleasing to God;

Rejoice, beloved son of the Mother of God;

Rejoice, O praise of the Holy Trinity, which makes the voice of the heart to resound;

Rejoice, holy Macarius, father of fathers!

Kontakion 13

(Three times)

O Holy Macarius, bearer of the Heavenly countenance, who by divine deeds hast shown us the truth of faith, enlighten our hearts with the knowledge of the Gospel. So we may cast off the darkness that besets us and receive the anointing of joy through the work of the Holy Spirit. Thus, Father, intercede for us from high, that we may not find ourselves unworthy and separated from your love in the fearful hour of our birth into eternity, but with you let us sing to the Most Holy Trinity: Alleluia!

Ikos 1 (repeated)

From Egypt and of a name with the Holy ancestors Abraham and Sarah, your parents embellished their lives with temperance and fasting, with prayers and vigils, with almsgiving and many other good deeds. Nonetheless, they were still united in spirit. For this reason the whole ray of the love of the Holy Trinity they lodged in their hearts, and the Holy Patriarch Abraham himself proclaimed to them in a vision the unbinding of their darkness by the joyful fruit which was to be the light of the worlds:

Rejoice, with your father the priest Abraham, in the joy of the vision of the holy patriarch;

Rejoice, that your parents, having had their wealth taken from them, hoped in the Lord;

Rejoice, for they were comforted by the divine light of good news;

Rejoice, for they did not delay to follow the will of the Lord;

Rejoice for they have moved according to God’s ordinance near the wilderness where you were to serve Him;

Rejoice, that your father was raised from the deadly disease with a steady hand;

Rejoice, for the angel of God has proclaimed to him mercy and blessings from above;

Rejoice, that the Holy Ghost has you as His abode;

Rejoice, for the name of happiness He has given you;

Rejoice, that the angelic life, fruitful of the salvation of many, has painted you;

Rejoice, for your parents gave thanks to the Lord with joy and trembling;

Rejoice, holy Macarius, father of fathers!

Kontakion 1 (repeated)

Blessed life in the world, you were worthy to dwell in the land of the meek, with the company of the faithful, Macarius, bearer of God, and dwelling in the wilderness as in a city, you received from Christ the grace of miracles. For this reason we honor you:

Rejoice, Holy Venerable Macarius, Father of fathers!
